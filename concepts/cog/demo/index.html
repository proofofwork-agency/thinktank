THE C⚙G

A capability-indexed unit of account for the intelligence economy. Not a coin. Not a chain. A ruler that doesn't bend — because the price of cognition falls ~10× a year, and every AI contract written in dollars is a hidden bet on that curve.

Stop paying 2024 prices for 2026 intelligence.
AI gets ~10× cheaper every year, but a fixed-dollar contract keeps charging you the old price anyway — on a $10k/month deal, that's $195,409 quietly lost over two years. Write the deal in cogs — a unit repriced daily to what AI actually costs — and your bill falls with the market. Automatically. No renegotiation.
⛽

Buy liters, not euros

Nobody signs up for “€80 of fuel per month forever” — you buy 50 liters at today's pump price. A cog is a liter of intelligence: the contract fixes the amount of AI work, and the price updates daily. Fuel gets cheaper → your bill drops by itself.

📞

The 50-cent minute

People kept paying €0.50/minute on old phone plans for years after calls became nearly free. Every fixed-dollar AI contract is that phone plan — except AI prices fall 10× per year, so it turns into a ripoff in months, not decades.

🇨🇱

Chile already proved it

Inflation made mortgages impossible to write, so since 1967 Chileans write them in the UF — a unit repriced daily by one published number. A whole economy runs on it. The cog is the same machine, pointed the other way: at prices that fall.
