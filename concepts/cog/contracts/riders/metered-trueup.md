# Metered Cog True-Up Rider

Template v0.1 — not legal advice

The recurring allowance is $included_cogs cogs of **$meter_name** per $period.
The Provider records usage in cogs before monetary resolution. Overages and
credits are trued up on $true_up_timing.

“COG-1 Fix” means eligible daily evidence. “Settlement Fix” means the
period-end fix selected under COG-SETTLE-1. A provisional anchor-fallback line
must identify its later true-up period and cannot become payable without the
required acknowledgement.
