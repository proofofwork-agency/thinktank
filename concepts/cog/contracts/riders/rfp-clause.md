# RFP Cog-Denomination Clause

Template v0.1 — not legal advice

For **$rfp_name**, bidders must state:

1. the fixed USD leg;
2. the cog quantity per service period;
3. the minimum acceptable evidence tier; and
4. any symmetric collar.

The final agreement must define “COG-1 Fix” and “Settlement Fix”, hash the
applicable unit specification, and disclose every unavailability-ladder rung on
the invoice. Payment rails may continue to settle in USD or USDC.
